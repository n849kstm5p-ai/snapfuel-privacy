Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapFuel ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application. We keep it simple and only collect what we need.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account or use SnapFuel:

  • Email address — used to create and manage your account
  • Payment information — processed securely through Google Play Billing when you subscribe to SnapFuel Pro. We never see or store your full payment details directly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account
  • To process and manage your SnapFuel Pro subscription
  • To restore your subscription status across devices and app reinstalls
  • To communicate with you about your account if needed

Third-Party Services

SnapFuel uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

🔥 Firebase (Google)

Used for account authentication and secure data storage. Your email and account data are stored in Firebase. Firebase Privacy Policy →

💳 Google Play Billing

Used to process SnapFuel Pro subscription payments. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. Google Payments Privacy Policy →

🤖 Google Gemini

Used to power AI food scanning features. When you scan a meal, the image is sent to Google Gemini for analysis. Images are not stored by us after analysis is complete. Gemini API Terms →

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

SnapFuel is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
